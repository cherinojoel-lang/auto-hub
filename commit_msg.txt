perf: Optimize performance bottlenecks

💡 What:
- Optimized \`AnimatedElement\` by bypassing the \`IntersectionObserver\` initialization, effect cleanup, and transition class assignments completely when \`priority={true}\`, returning a static wrapper directly. This prevents unnecessary ref calculations and delay timeouts for above-the-fold content.
- Restructured \`filterVehicles\` to use early-exit boolean logic (single \`.filter\` with \`&&\` chain) instead of chained \`.filter\` array allocations.
- Fixed \`<Image>\` in \`VehicleInventorySection\` and \`VehiclesPage\` to explicitly provide \`loading="eager"\` and \`fetchPriority="high"\` for above-the-fold LCP elements.

🎯 Why:
- The \`AnimatedElement\` component previously executed its entire effect hook and assigned invisible CSS classes even for \`priority={true}\` elements, causing unnecessary render cycles and delaying the LCP for critical hero content.
- Chaining multiple \`.filter()\` array methods creates multiple intermediate arrays, causing memory bloat and GC pauses on large vehicle datasets. A single boolean chain prevents these intermediate allocations.
- Top vehicles implicitly fell back to lazy loading or lacked \`fetchPriority\` hints, delaying their appearance on screen.

📊 Impact:
- **LCP:** Significantly improved by unblocking priority images and stripping JS delay logic from critical elements.
- **Memory/CPU:** Reduced garbage collection overhead and intermediate array creation by collapsing 6 \`.filter()\` loops into 1.
- **Render blocking:** Reduced by bypassing intersection observers on priority components.

🔬 Measurement:
- Local tests confirm priority components now render immediately in their final state without intermediate opacity/translation classes.
- Unit and integration tests in CI continue to pass.
